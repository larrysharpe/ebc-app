# Stewardship & Finance

How Ebenezer receives, records, and stewards the church's financial resources.

## Purpose

Honor God through faithful handling of tithes and offerings, transparent accounting, and wise budgeting so ministry is funded and the congregation can give with confidence.

## Key activities

- Receive gifts during worship and online
- Count, document, and deposit offerings (Realm batch entry)
- Nehemiah Project fundraising for sanctuary completion
- Annual giving statements via Realm (email or print)
- Trustee oversight of budget and property

## Processes

### Recording contributions

1. **Online gifts** — Posted through Realm Giving / website platform
2. **Cash and checks** — Batch entry in Realm after worship count team process (TBD — document count team roster)
3. **Cash App** — Reconcile to donor with name in submission notes

### Mail contributions

Address to Trustees; no cash by mail per [give page](https://ebenezerbc.org/other/give).

### Annual member statements

Generated through **Realm** — timing and distribution TBD with finance staff.

- **Confidentiality** — Individual giving known only to authorized finance personnel and pastoral staff as needed
- **Dual control** — Offering counts TBD — document with trustee/deacon leadership
