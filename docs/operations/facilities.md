# Facilities

Buildings, grounds, rentals, and capital projects at Ebenezer Baptist Church.

Source: [ebenezerbc.org/facilities](https://ebenezerbc.org/facilities) · [Nehemiah Project](https://ebenezerbc.org/connect/nehemiah-project-ministry)

## Purpose

Provide safe, welcoming spaces for worship and ministry; manage community rentals; advance sanctuary completion.

## Campus overview

| Space | Capacity / use | Status |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| **Family Life Center (FLC)** | Gym/banquet ~500; commercial kitchen; classrooms up to ~200 | Active — rentals, ministries, counseling |
| **Chapel** | Current Sunday worship | Active — primary worship entrance |
| **New sanctuary** | Future worship space | Under construction (Nehemiah Phase III) |
| **Parking** | Near FLC and Chapel | Use Chapel entrance during sanctuary build |

### FLC amenities

- Full-court gymnasium / banquet room
- Adjoining commercial kitchen
- Classrooms for various group sizes
- COUNT ME IN signup boxes throughout FLC

**Uses:** Weddings, receptions, banquets, meetings, funerals, repasts, basketball, church activities. Setup and breakdown by facility staff.

## Nehemiah Project (capital)

Scripture-based building campaign since **1995** (Nehemiah 2:18).

| Phase | Outcome |
|-------|---------|
| I–II | Eleven acres; Family Life Center built and occupied |
| III | New sanctuary structure + Chapel (Chapel inaugural Sep 5, 2021) |
| Ongoing | Debt paydown; complete sanctuary and administrative offices |

Motto: *Building on the Rock!*

## Rental process

1. Call Church Administrator (703-494-2669) or Facility Usage Manager (703-307-0207)
2. Schedule walk-through
3. Facility staff handles table/chair setup and breakdown
