# Congregation & Membership

How Ebenezer Baptist Church knows, welcomes, and shepherds its people.

## Purpose

Maintain a healthy church body by welcoming visitors, discipling believers, and caring for families across the membership lifecycle — from first visit through active service and, when needed, transitions away.

## Membership categories

Define how the church classifies people in the congregation. Confirm with leadership:

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Visitor** | Attending, not yet committed to membership |
| **Regular attender** | Consistent presence, not yet a member |
| **Member** | Covenanted member in good standing |
| **Inactive** | No longer attending; pastoral follow-up as appropriate |

## Key activities

- Welcome and register first-time guests
- Follow up with visitors after initial visits
- Conduct new member orientation / membership class
- Maintain accurate household and contact information
- Coordinate care for members (illness, bereavement, pastoral needs)
- Process transfers of membership in and out

## Policies

- **Privacy** — Member information is shared only on a need-to-know basis. Ministry leaders receive contact info for their areas, not unrestricted access to the full directory. Full rules: [data practices](../governance/data-practices.md) and the app pages `/legal/privacy` and `/legal/terms`.
- **Children & youth** — Kids are full **Person** records (so they can appear on youth choir, etc.), linked to a **household** as role **Child**. **Birthday** drives age gates:
  - **Under 13** — no app login; contact guardians via the household
  - **Junior (13–17)** — may receive limited app roles: Choir Member, Band Member, Volunteer (needs an email)
  - **18+** — full directory-assignable roles
- Prefer guardian contact for younger children; juniors may use their own or a parent-managed email.
- **Names** — Use the **suffix** field for Jr., III, IV, etc. — do not embed suffixes in last name.
