# Music & Choir Ministry

How Ebenezer plans worship music, equips the choir, and maintains its song library.

Source: [website ministries](https://ebenezerbc.org/connect) · Fall Fresh Choir Workshop · Youth Choir (website)

## Purpose

Support worship through organized music ministry — planning songs for services and events, equipping singers with lyrics and parts, and helping musicians lead confidently.

### Combined choir engagements

When more than one chapel choir sings together (Christmas, revival, Fall Fresh, etc.), use the **Combined Choir** entry in Choir setup / choir plans. It is **not** part of the monthly Sunday rotation.

1. Edit Combined Choir and add the singers taking part (roster).
2. Create the choir plan with choir = **Combined Choir**.
3. Set **Occasion** to name the groups (e.g. “Youth + Senior”).

## Key activities

- Select music for Sunday worship and special services
- Distribute song lists, lyrics, and **choir parts** to singers before rehearsal
- Provide chord charts, lead sheets, or **how-to-play** guidance for musicians
- Maintain a **song catalog** — titles, keys, themes, season/liturgical use
- Lead rehearsals (choir, praise team)
- Coordinate with [Media Ministry](https://ebenezerbc.org/connect/media-ministry) for AV / livestream
- Seasonal programs — Fall Fresh workshop, Christmas, Easter, revival, VBS

## Processes

### Planning music for a service

1. Director selects service date — app shows **default choir** for that Sunday of the month
2. If swapped, mark `scheduleOverride` and note why (e.g. Men's off Father's Day)
3. Schedule **Saturday practice** in chapel (typical 8–10 AM; time may shift via text thread)
3. Build set list — welcome, worship, congregational hymn, offering, sermonic, last song slots; assign soloists and section notes
4. Send plan to choir by email/text (EBC APP: `/music/plans` — preview matches Sister Stewart format)
5. Optional **Friday evening** practice for leads and section leaders
6. Sunday call time (e.g. 8:30 AM) — coordinate sound with media ministry
7. Post-service thank-you message to choir, musicians, graduates, adults, media team

## Policies

- **Copyright** — Only distribute lyrics and sheet music the church is licensed to use. Document licensing approach with leadership.
- **Access** — Choir parts and musician charts restricted to music ministry and assigned singers/musicians.
- Worship selections align with pastor and [doctrine](../strategy/mission-and-vision.md).
