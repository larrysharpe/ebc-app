## COUNT ME IN program

EBC's church-wide volunteer initiative. Members commit skills across ministry and mission; church tracks volunteer hours in a volunteer database.

### How to sign up

1. Sign up between services at the rear of the church
2. Complete a form and place in **Count Me In! boxes** in the FLC
3. Email completed form to countmein@ebenezerbc.org

Coordinators follow up with volunteers.
