## Activity requests (in-app)

Ministry and staff event creation in EBC APP includes a digitized **Activity Request** (People, Kitchen, Media, Floor plan, Coordination, Review). Paper PDF remains available from the [Resource Center](https://ebenezerbc.org/resources/resource-center) for reference; prefer the in-app form for church events.

- Ministry leaders: **Save draft** or **Submit for approval**
- Approvers: **office staff** or **trustees** (Approve / Return on Events and ministry calendars)
- Lead time: 90 days ideal tip; under 4 weeks flexible warning; under 3 days emergency path (contact the office)
- Optional **Voice coach** on the event form (Chrome/Edge): when creating an event, choose yes/no (not a form step). Check **Remember my choice** to save a personal preference under Account → Preferences (ask each time / always / never).
