# Communications

> **Status:** Leadership case first.  
> Use **[Case for Communications](../strategy/case-for-communications.md)** with pastor and boards.  
> Do not build heavy process or software until leadership says yes to a named team.

## Working model (once affirmed)

People own the church’s voice. **AI is on the team as the expert** — drafts, options, gap-finding — the way a strong writer/researcher would. The lead (and pastor for sensitive items) always decide what publishes. No auto-send. No prayer, giving, or personal contact data in AI prompts.

## Until then

Today the work is **real but scattered**: office, Media, ministry leaders, and the pastor all carry pieces of the public voice.
