# Social Media & Community Presence

How Ebenezer manages public social channels, plans content, and shows up at community events in Woodbridge and Prince William County.

**Parent:** Ownership TBD — see [Case for Communications](../strategy/case-for-communications.md). Today Media handles much of the public tech face; office handles inquiries.

## Purpose

Extend EBC's mission **beyond the walls** — consistent social presence, timely content tied to church life, and intentional participation in community events where the congregation can serve, witness, and invite.

Social work runs inside the Communications **AI Discover → Execute → Evaluate** loop ([communications.md](communications.md)): AI surfaces what to post, drafts captions, and scores results; humans approve before anything goes public.

Aligns with: *Putting the Family Back Together* and outreach through [missionary ministry](ministries.md).

## Processes

### Publishing a social post

Target workflow in EBC APP (AI-first):

1. Opportunity appears in Communications **Discover** (or Accept from social suggestions)
2. **Execute** — AI drafts caption; attach media from Media Ministry or approved library
3. Select platforms and scheduled time
4. Approval if required (pastor / Communications lead for sensitive topics)
5. Publish or schedule via platform APIs — **human click required**
6. Log published URL; feed outcome into weekly **Evaluate**

### Content suggestions (Discover)

System proposes ideas when (feeds Communications Discover):

- Church [event](events.md) is 7–14 days out
- Weekly worship reminder (Thursday–Saturday)
- Seasonal / liturgical moments (Easter, Christmas, Homecoming)
- Sermon uploaded to YouTube (clip or quote graphic)
- Gap in posting schedule detected
- Approved [announcement](../engineering/requirements/features/communications/announcement-workflow.md) ready for social
- Weekly **Evaluate** flags underused content pillars

Human always reviews before publish — **no auto-post without approval**.

## Policies

- **Approval** — Pastor or designee approves posts on doctrine, politics, and crisis; Communications handles day-to-day worship/event posts (TBD thresholds).
- **Photo consent** — Follow church policy for identifiable members and children in public posts.
- **Politics** — Church may share community information (e.g. voter education) when leadership approves; avoid partisan endorsement (case-by-case with pastor).
- **Credentials** — Social platform tokens stored securely; only authorized roles connect accounts.
- **Copyright** — Music and video clips must respect platform and content rights.
