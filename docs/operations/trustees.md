# Trustee Ministry & Duties

How Ebenezer's Trustee Ministry stewards property, finances, and operational responsibilities.

Source: [Trustee Ministry](https://ebenezerbc.org/connect/church-leadership/trustee-ministry) · [Governance](../governance/leadership.md)

## Purpose

The Trustee Ministry manages the church's material possessions and financial matters with spiritual sensitivity — property, obligations, assets, and the operational discipline that supports God's mission at EBC.

## Mission & vision (from church)

- **Mission:** Be good stewards of EBC, the budget, operations, and resources God has entrusted to the board.
- **Vision:** Financially and operationally sustainable with best-in-class practices.
- **Values:** Clarity, consistency, fiscal discipline, small wins toward the big goal.

## Key activities

- Oversee church property and physical assets (with [facilities](facilities.md))
- Steward budget, expenses, and financial obligations (with [giving](giving.md) / Realm)
- Support Nehemiah Project / capital completion (with [ministries](ministries.md))
- Trustee board meetings — agenda, minutes, action items
- Assign and track **trustee duties** — recurring and one-time responsibilities
- Vendor and maintenance oversight (HVAC, cleaning, etc.)
- Participate in Sunday weather cancellation decisions (with Pastor and Deacon Chair)
- Review facility rental and major contracts

## Trustee duty categories

| Category | Examples |
