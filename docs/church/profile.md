## Historical notes

- First Negro church established in this part of Northern Virginia (1881).
- **Founder:** Rev. Lewis Henry Bailey, former slave, Ebenezer Baptist Church of Alexandria, VA.
- **Family Life Center** dedicated June 2000; **Chapel** inaugural service September 5, 2021.
- **Nehemiah Project** capital campaign since 1995 — new sanctuary and facilities ongoing.
