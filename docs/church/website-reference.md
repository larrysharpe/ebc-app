## Visitor experience

From [New to EBC](https://ebenezerbc.org/about/new-to-ebc):

1. Greeted by Greeter & Usher Ministry at Chapel entrance
2. Parking near FLC and Chapel (sanctuary construction — use Chapel entrance)
3. Worship in Chapel; ushers assist with seating
4. Meet Pastor and 1st Lady after service (front of Chapel / narthex)
5. Prayer requests: ask usher for deacon or email prayerrequests@ebenezerbc.org

No dress code — “concerned with Christ in you, not clothes on you.”
