# Communications playbook — team, platforms, metrics

**Audience:** Pastor, Joint Board, Media, the people who will actually staff this  
**Status:** Working draft. The [case for Communications](case-for-communications.md) still needs a leadership yes. This is what we build **after** that yes.  
**Date:** 2026-08-23

The product is **original pictures and video of this church family** — not recycled 360p pulpit tape, not stock, not the August announcements deck. That work needs a crew. Two people can hold the clipboard. They cannot also shoot, edit, post, run the sign, and keep the website honest.

Names below marked **placeholder** are fictional seats for recruiting. They are not members. Real names live in the EBC APP roster, not this file.

---

## What we are trying to do

**Putting the Family Back Together** in public: a guest in Woodbridge should know who we are, when to come, and that they are welcome — from the roadside, the phone, and the Chapel door.

| Outcome (90 days) | What “good” looks like |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| One church voice | Same Sunday time, same event dates, on every channel |
| Original media every week | New photos or vertical clips from **this** Sunday, not last year’s stream |
| Guests can find us | Website, Google, Facebook, and the sign all say **Sunday 9:45 AM** |
| Pastor is not the bulletin board | Requests in by a deadline; he only sees doctrine, crisis, and major vision |
| Nobody burns out | Sunday shooters rotate. No one is livestream *and* phone-vertical the same hour |

Sunday worship time for **guest-facing** copy is **9:45 AM** (confirmed for the board). `docs/church/profile.md` and events still say 10:00 AM — Communications owns getting those docs and the website in line.

## How the work splits

Media already has a job: **sound, slides, livestream camera, VNNOX player.** Do not put original social video on the same operator.

| Team | Owns | Does not own |
|------|------|----------------|
| **Communications** | Words, cadence, which platforms, captions, bulletin/website notes, social publish, photo consent log, metrics | Doctrine, crisis, the live mix |
| **Media (AV)** | Livestream, sanctuary cameras, slides, audio, putting approved files on the sign | Writing the message |
| **Field crew** (shared, scheduled by Communications) | Phone-vertical video and stills in the aisle, at the door, choir faces | Standing in the livestream shot |
| **Pastor** | Teaching, crisis, politics, funerals, anything with a name that could hurt | Day-to-day event copy |

AI stays the **draft expert** from the case: first pass on captions and checklists. A person always clicks publish. No prayer lists, giving amounts, or pastoral notes in prompts.

## Original media — the weekly machine

Livestream stays the **archive**. It is 640×360 and aimed at the pulpit. Shorts and Reels will not grow from that tape. Field crew films **beside** the live camera, never instead of it.

### Shot list (every Sunday, 9:30–12:15)

Phone **vertical**. 1080p if the phone allows. Stay out of the livestream.

| Clip | Length | Who | Notes |
|------|--------|-----|--------|
| Aisle walk | 8s | Video A | Toward the cross. No talking. |
| Choir / praise team | 10s | Video A | Faces and hands, not a balcony smear |
| Door welcome | 8s | Video B | Greeter. Ask first. |
| One sentence | 12s | Video B or pastor | “Come as you are. Sunday 9:45. All are welcome.” Look at the phone. |
| Stills | 15–25 frames | Photo | Door, pews, choir, one wide. Delete kids unless consent is on file. |

Do not film giving plates, Cash App slides, healing lists, or duty deacons. Those are not guest media.

### What ships from that shoot (same week)

| Asset | Size | Where it goes |
|-------|------|----------------|
| One 15s reel | 1080×1920 | Facebook Reels **and** YouTube Shorts (same file) |
| One feed still | 1080×1080 | Facebook post (and Instagram feed when that account is live) |
| One story still | 1080×1920 | Facebook / Instagram Stories |
| Sign file | 1672×941 | VNNOX — Media publishes |
| Website/bulletin line | text | Calvin’s seat |

Editor cuts by **Tuesday night**. Publisher posts **Thursday** (worship invite) and **Saturday** (tomorrow / live link). Monday is scorecard, not more production.

When the church films Come As You Are or Revival, drop the new clips in `scripts/media-campaigns/` and rebuild. Stock and 360p archives are stand-ins only.

## Weekly rhythm

```
Mon   Scorecard (15 min). Lead + editor.
Tue   Editor finishes reel + stills. AI drafts captions. Lead approves ordinary copy.
Wed   Bible study reminder (text + one still). Website/bulletin match.
Thu   Publisher posts Sunday invite (Reel + feed). Facebook Event is correct.
Sat   Publisher posts “tomorrow 9:45” + live link. Media confirms stream + sign.
Sun   Field crew shoots. Media runs live. Communications does not hover the pulpit.
```

Request deadline for Sunday morning announcements: **Wednesday 5 PM**. After that it waits a week unless the pastor says it is urgent.

## Metrics

Measure **faithfulness and clarity**, not likes. No names, no giving amounts, no prayer text in the tracker.

### Weekly scorecard (print this)

Fill it Monday. Yes / no unless a number is asked. Ten minutes.

| # | Check | Yes / No or number |
|---|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | Sunday time said **9:45 AM** on website, Facebook, sign, and this week’s reel | |
| 2 | Next public event has the **same date** on website and Facebook Event | |
| 3 | We posted **one original** reel or still from **this** church (not stock, not 360p pulpit) | |
| 4 | YouTube live started and the archive is up | |
| 5 | Field crew shot (or a named sub did) | |
| 6 | No child posted without consent on file | |
| 7 | No healing list, bereavement names, or duty roster went public | |
| 8 | Pastor was not asked for a new announce **after** Wednesday 5 PM | |
| 9 | Open seats: how many of the 8 core seats had a person this week? | /8 |
| 10 | Guest-facing mistake we had to correct (0 is the goal) | |

A week with 8–10 yeses is a good week. A week with a beautiful Reel and the wrong time is a failed week.

### Monthly numbers (five only)

Keep a single sheet. Aggregates only.

| Metric | Source | Target (first 90 days) | Why |
|--------|--------|------------------------|-----|
| **Original packs shipped** | Scorecard #3 count | 4 / month (one a week) | Proves the crew exists |
| **Sunday time mismatches** | Scorecard #1 failures | 0 | Guests |
| **YouTube lives completed** | Studio | 4 / month | Media partnership |
| **Shorts / Reels posted** | Facebook + YouTube | 4 / month | Same file, two places |
| **Google profile complete** | Business Profile | Hours, photos, 9:45, phone — all set | Strangers |

Optional later (do not start here): Facebook Event responses, Shorts average views. Never: who watched, who gave, who is on a prayer list.

### 90-day score

Leadership can judge the experiment on this, not on follower count.

| Goal | Pass |
|------|------|
| Crew | At least **5 of 8** core seats named (real people in the roster) |
| Original media | **10** Sunday packs in 12 weeks (two weeks off for illness is allowed) |
| Platforms | Google claimed. Facebook Page URL written down. Instagram opened **only** if Photo + Video A are filled |
| Accuracy | **Zero** public 10:00 AM leftovers on website, sign, or Facebook |
| Pastor load | He can name the request deadline without being asked |

## What we will not post

From church life, not from theory:

- Named healing or sick lists  
- Bereavement with family names unless the family asked  
- Duty deacons / trustees as a public graphic  
- Watermarked stock and other churches’ flyers  
- Children without written consent  
- Giving amounts, Cash App handles as the hero, plates in close-up  
- Culture-war or protest imagery  
- Last year’s revival video with this year’s preacher names  

When in doubt, the lead holds it. When it could hurt a family, the pastor holds it.

## First 30 days (recruiting, not posting more)

1. Leadership **yes** on the named team ([case](case-for-communications.md)).  
2. You and your wife pick **lead** and **editor**. Write your real names in the roster.  
3. COUNT ME IN ask: four Sunday seats — photo, video A, video B, publisher. Use the placeholder table so people see a real job, not “help with social.”  
4. Claim **Google Business Profile**. Put 9:45 AM and three original stills on it.  
5. Confirm Facebook Page URL and login owner.  
6. Fix profile/events/website **10:00 → 9:45** wherever guests read.  
7. One Sunday shot list, even if only two phones that week. Ship one reel from **that** Sunday.

Do not open Instagram or Nextdoor in week one. Do not promise TikTok.
