# Mission & Vision

Strategic direction for Ebenezer Baptist Church of Woodbridge, VA.

Source: [ebenezerbc.org/about](https://ebenezerbc.org/about) · [doctrine](https://ebenezerbc.org/about/doctrine)

## Mission

**Putting the Family Back Together**

Ebenezer is a vibrant congregation dedicated to restoring families and deepening our relationship with God. Ministry focuses on rebuilding family values, reestablishing the man's role within the family, and fostering a stronger connection with Jesus Christ.

## Theme

**We're Marching to Zion!**

## Vision

With God, **all things are possible**. Pastor Lundy's vision includes:

- Preaching and teaching that reaches every age and background
- Leading God's people in righteous living, tithe giving, and holy worship
- A **larger sanctuary and Family Life Center** (including gymnasium) for fellowship and worship
- Ministries that train and restore men to their God-given role in the family
- Enlarging the church family to bless Occoquan, Lake Ridge, and the wider community

## Strategic priorities (from public messaging)

- New sanctuary completion (Nehemiah Project Phase III)
- Family restoration and men's discipleship (JAMM, Mountain Men, counseling)
- Christian education and Bible literacy
- Community outreach (missionary, prison, food pantry)
- Digital ministry (YouTube, livestream, mobile app)
